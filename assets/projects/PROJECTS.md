# Projects build info

Every project must contain this files:

- ```card.png```: A short cover, recommended resolution 720x256.
- ```icon.png```: A small icon, recommended resolution 256x256.

<br>
Every project can aslo have this optional files:

- ```screenshot_x.png```: A simple screenshot of the project (Where X is the ammount of screenshots), recommended resolution 1366x768.
- ```background.mp4```: A background video that playbacks automatically without audio, recommended resolution 1366x768.
